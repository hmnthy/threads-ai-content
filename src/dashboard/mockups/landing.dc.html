01 · Problem

150 posts and 674 answers to followers. Threads can't say what they add up to.

@thydilammuon writes for Vietnamese students and workers in France: alternance, job hunting, everyday life. Running the channel raises three questions the Threads app has no screen for.

Q1 Which posts did well for this channel, not in general?
Threads showsViews, likes, replies, reposts and quotes, one post at a time. MissingA reference point. 3,000 views is a strong day for one channel and a weak one for another.
Q2 What does the channel write about, and which of it lands?
Threads showsPosts in time order. MissingTopics read from the text itself, written in Vietnamese mixed with French and English.
Q3 What has the author already answered?
Threads showsReplies inside each post's own thread. MissingA way to search 674 answers across every post.
02 · How it solves

One layer per question. Each one feeds the next.

Follow the hero post through all three. Topics from layer 2 become the comparison groups in layer 1. Clean text and topic from layer 2 become the documents of layer 3.
